Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quests Bay Pte. Ltd. (“Quests”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, discloses and protects personal data when you visit this website, contact us, or work with us. It is written to meet the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 of Singapore (PDPA) and, for people in Japan, the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI).

1. Who we are

Quests Bay Pte. Ltd. is a company incorporated in Singapore and is responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

  • Address: 68 Circular Road, #02-01, Singapore 049422
  • Contact: our contact form

The name of our representative director is available on request.

2. Personal data we collect

When you contact us

Our contact form does not send your details to our servers. It opens your own email app or service with a message addressed to us. We receive only what you choose to send, typically your name, email address, company name, budget range and the details of your project, plus anything else you include in your email.

When we work with you

If you or your organisation become a client, partner or supplier, we collect business contact details (such as name, job title, company, email address and phone number) and the information needed to deliver and manage the engagement.

When you visit this website

Like most websites, the services that host and support this site automatically receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, the pages you request and the date and time. We do not use analytics tools, advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this website.

Personal data we handle for our clients

In client projects, we may handle personal data on a client’s behalf, such as data about the client’s customers or employees. We handle it only on the client’s instructions and under our agreement with that client. That processing is governed by the agreement and the client’s own privacy notice, not by this policy.

3. How we use personal data

We use personal data for these purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and arrange meetings
  • To prepare proposals, enter into contracts and deliver our services
  • To manage our relationship with clients, partners and suppliers, including billing
  • To send information about our services when you have asked for it (you can opt out at any time)
  • To keep this website and our systems secure and working properly
  • To meet legal, regulatory and accounting obligations, and to establish or defend legal claims

We will not use personal data for a new purpose without telling you and, where required, obtaining your consent.

4. Cookies and browser storage

This website does not set its own cookies. It stores your chosen language in your browser (local storage) so the site opens in that language next time, and briefly uses session storage to load updated content. Neither is used to identify or track you, and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Some third-party services shown on this site may set their own cookies, as described in the next section.

6. How we share personal data

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as email, cloud storage and accounting services, under contracts that require them to protect the data and use it only for our purposes
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, auditors and insurers, under duties of confidentiality
  • Authorities when required by law, regulation or court order
  • A buyer or successor if our business is reorganised, merged or sold, subject to this policy
  • Anyone else with your consent

7. International transfers

We are based in Singapore, and our service providers may store or process personal data in other countries, including the United States. When we transfer personal data outside Singapore, we take steps to ensure it receives a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA, for example through contractual commitments.

For people in Japan: when we provide personal data to a third party in a foreign country, we do so in line with the APPI. On request, we will provide information about the country concerned, its personal information protection system and the measures the recipient takes.

8. How we protect personal data

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, use, loss or disclosure. These include limiting access to people who need it, requiring confidentiality from staff and contractors, using reputable service providers with strong security, and encrypting data in transit where appropriate.

Where we store personal data with providers in other countries, we do so after checking the personal data protection systems of those countries.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. Please do not send highly sensitive or confidential information through ordinary email until we have agreed how to exchange it.

9. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law (for example, for tax and accounting records). When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it has been used or disclosed
  • Correct personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Stop using or delete your personal data, or stop providing it to third parties
  • Withdraw consent you have given. We will tell you the likely consequences before acting on it.
  • Stop sending you information about our services

To make a request, please contact us through our contact form. We may need to verify your identity before we respond, and we will reply within a reasonable time. Where the law allows, we may charge a reasonable fee for access requests, and we will tell you the amount in advance.

11. Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Links to other websites

This website may link to other websites, such as our YouTube channel. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If the changes are significant, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.

14. Contact us

For questions, requests or complaints about this policy or our handling of personal data, please contact our Data Protection Officer.
