What it does

It keeps score and calls it out

After every rally it calls the score, like “1-0-2”. It keeps track of who serves, so nobody has to stop and recount. When the game ends, the score goes into your history.

Side-out and rally scoring. Calls are in English, and you can switch them to Japanese.

Every shot on the court, misses included

Tap yourself once in the video and Pickle Up draws each shot you hit. Shots that land get a circle; outs and nets get a red ×. Filter by shot and the differences show up in numbers. Maybe your drops land and your volleys keep finding the net.

An AI coach that reads your numbers and tells you what to practise

The AI coach reads your match numbers and shot patterns, then tells you what went well and what to fix. If 3 of your 8 volleys went into the net, the Practice tab has drills like Volley hands battle. There are 25 drills, sorted by USA Pickleball skill level, with the ones for your level first.

How it works

  1. 1

    Put your phone behind the court

    Behind the baseline, as high as you can. You need all four corners in view, plus the server waiting on your side. A tripod or a fence mount makes it easy.

  2. 2

    Play as usual

    Tap Start recording and play. The score is kept and called after every rally.

  3. 3

    Look back after the match

    Tap yourself once on the match screen to get your shot map. The Insights tab adds up your weeks and months.

Your videos stay on your phone

  • Videos never leave it

    Videos are saved only on your phone. Our servers only handle the body and ball movement worked out from the video.

  • No sign-up

    No account needed. To move your data to a new phone, create an account and sync.

  • No ads, no tracking

    There are no tracking tools in the app, and your information never leaves it for advertising or anything like it.

Questions

Where do I put my phone?

Behind the court, with all four corners and the server on your side in view. The app’s filming guide shows an example.

Where are my videos kept?

In the app’s own storage on your phone. Videos are never sent to our servers.

How accurate is the analysis?

It is estimated from one phone’s video, so landings on the far side and ball heights are off by a little. Shots it cannot judge with confidence are not drawn; they are counted as unclear.

Is it free?

Recording, automatic scoring and calls, and the drills are free. Shot analysis is free for your first video. With Premium there is no limit.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone and Android versions are on the way. We will post here when they are out.
