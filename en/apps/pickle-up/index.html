Features

Film the match. The app does the rest.

01

Just film it.The scorekeeps itself.

Set your phone down and play as usual. Pickle Up splits the rallies, keeps the score, and calls it out the official way after every rally. No more arguing over the score, no more scribbled notes.

  • Rallies and score, recorded for you
  • Score called out loud (English or Japanese)
  • Tells a match from practice

02

Every shot you hit,on the court

Tap yourself once in the video. Every shot you hit is drawn from where you hit it to where it landed. In or out, and whether it was volleyed back, at a glance.

  • Where you hit and where it landed
  • In rate and volley share, counted for you
  • By match, week or month

03

Spot your habits,shot by shot

Serves, returns, drops, dinks and drives. Split your shots by kind and compare their paths. Habits like short returns or long drops show up in the picture and the numbers.

  • Serves, returns, dinks and more
  • Landing spread, drawn on the court
  • A 0–100 score for each kind of shot

04

See the height in 3D.Turn it with a finger.

Shots are drawn in 3D, with an estimate of how far above the net each one passed. Check whether your dinks float or your drives stay low, from any angle.

  • Shot height in 3D
  • Height from the net (median)
  • Turn and zoom to any angle

05

An AI coach that tells youwhat to fix next

From your match numbers and shot patterns, the AI coach tells you what went well and narrows what to work on down to three points. On practice videos, it looks at your form too.

  • What went well, and what to fix
  • Concrete tips you can try next time
  • For matches, practice and weekly recaps

06

Drills that matchyour level

25 drills, sorted by USA Pickleball skill level. Dinks, drops, volleys and more, each with a court diagram and steps, with the ones for your level suggested first.

  • 25 drills, by skill level
  • Court diagrams and step-by-step
  • Practice log and weekly goals

Privacy

Your videos stay on your phone

Your videos are kept on your phone only. All of the video analysis happens on the device, and videos are never sent to our servers.

  • Analysed on your phone

    Finding players and the ball, splitting rallies and keeping the score all happen on the device.

  • Videos never leave it

    Videos are kept in the app’s own storage. They are shared only when you choose to.

  • No sign-up needed

    No name or email needed. Create an account only if you want to move your records to a new phone.

How it works

Three steps, starting with your next match

  1. 1

    Set it behind the court

    Put your phone behind the baseline, as high as you can. Once all four corners and the server waiting behind the near line are in view, you are ready.

  2. 2

    Film the match

    Tap Start recording and play as usual. The score is kept and called out for you.

  3. 3

    See your shots and tips

    Go through your shots on the match screen and in the Insights tab, and read the AI coach’s advice.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How should I film?

From behind the court, with all four corners and the server on the near side in view. The app’s filming guide shows where to put your phone.

Where are my videos kept?

In the app’s own storage on your phone. Videos are never sent to our servers.

How accurate is the analysis?

Everything is estimated from a single phone’s video, so landings on the far side and ball heights have some error. Shots that cannot be judged with enough certainty are not drawn; they are counted as unclear.

Is it free?

Recording, automatic scoring and calls, and the drills are free. Shot analysis is free for your first video, and unlimited with Premium.

Which phones does it run on?

Versions for iPhone and Android are on the way. We will announce them on this page.
