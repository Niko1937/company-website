Features

Film the match. The app does the rest.

01

Set it up and play. Rallies and score, automatic.

pickle-up finds where each rally starts and ends, keeps the score, and tells a match from practice. It can call the score out loud, so you can keep your eyes on the ball.

  • Splits the rallies for you
  • Keeps and calls the score
  • Tells matches from practice

02

Your shots, on the court

Tap yourself once in the video. Each shot you hit is drawn from where you hit it to where it landed on the other side. Look over a week or a month and your patterns show.

  • Where you hit and where it landed
  • Filter by in, out and volleys
  • By week, month, match or rally

03

See the height in 3D. Turn it with a finger.

Shots are drawn in 3D, with an estimate of how far above the net each one passed. Check whether your dinks float or your drives stay low, in centimetres.

  • Shot height in 3D
  • Height from the net (median)
  • Turn and zoom to any angle

04

Your practice, at a glance

See the time you practised each day, by week or by month. Practice you log by hand and matches you film are counted together.

  • Practice time by week and month
  • The days you played, on a calendar
  • Practice log and reminders

Privacy

Your videos stay on your phone

Every video is analysed on your phone. Neither your videos nor the results are ever sent to our servers.

  • Analysed on your phone

    Finding players and the ball, and working out every shot, happens on the device, offline.

  • Videos never leave it

    Videos are kept in the app’s own storage. They are shared only when you choose to.

  • No sign-up

    Start right away, no account needed.

How it works

Three steps, starting with your next match

  1. 1

    Set it behind the court

    Put your phone behind the baseline, as high as you can. Once all four corners and the server waiting behind the near line are in view, you are ready.

  2. 2

    Film the match

    Tap Start recording and play as usual.

  3. 3

    Look back at your shots

    Open the Insights tab to go through your shots, match by match and rally by rally.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How should I film?

From behind the court, with all four corners and the server on the near side in view. The app’s filming guide shows where to put your phone.

Where are my videos kept?

In the app’s own storage on your phone. We never upload them.

How accurate is the analysis?

Everything is estimated from a single phone’s video, so landings on the far side and ball heights have some error. Shots that cannot be judged with enough certainty are not drawn; they are counted as unclear.

Which phones does it run on?

Versions for iPhone and Android are on the way. We will announce them on this page.
