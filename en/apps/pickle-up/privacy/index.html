Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Quests Bay Pte. Ltd. (“we”) handles information in the Pickle Up app for smartphones (“the app”). For how we handle personal data in general, see our privacy policy.

1. In short

  • The videos you film stay on your phone. We never upload them.
  • Videos are analysed on your phone too: body positions, the ball, rallies and the score.
  • Your records and analysis results are backed up to an account. You don’t have to sign in: when needed, the app creates an anonymous account for you (section 3).
  • Only when you use the AI coach, the app sends numbers worked out on your phone and up to 4 still images of your form. The images are deleted as soon as the advice is ready (section 3).
  • The app sends anonymous statistics, such as how many times a day each feature is used. You can turn them off in Settings at any time (section 3).
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising or tracking.

2. What stays on your phone

The following is kept in the app’s own storage on your phone and is never sent to our servers:

  • The videos you film, with their sound
  • Video thumbnails
  • Skeleton and ball analysis files (used to replay skeletons without the video)
  • Voice-over recordings
  • Settings for this phone (appearance, language, units, notifications, haptics and so on)

To tell players apart, the analysis uses where people stand and the colours of their clothes. It does not recognise faces or identify anyone.

When you start or stop recording by voice (“Start” and “Stop”), your voice is recognised on this phone only. It is never sent anywhere or recorded.

3. What we send, and why

Your account

The first time you use a feature that needs our servers, such as the AI coach, a purchase or backing up your records, the app quietly creates an anonymous account. No name or email address is needed. The key used to sign in is kept only in your phone’s keychain (keystore). On iPhone, the key stays even if you delete the app, so reinstalling picks up the same account.

You can create a full account at any time, if you wish, with email, Google, Apple or a passkey: go to Settings › Account › Create account / Sign in. We then receive what is needed to sign you in, such as your email address. We use your email address for verification codes and signing in.

Backing up your records

Your records and analysis results are saved to your account. Create an account and sign in, and they move to a new phone with you. We save:

  • Video details (date, length, notes, favourites, the paddle used, court corner positions and so on). The video files themselves are never sent
  • Analysis results (rallies, the score, shot positions and heights, and which player is you)
  • Match and practice records, scene notes, drawings, landing spots you tagged, tags and folders
  • Your profile (name, level, dominant hand, goals and so on), weekly goals, and your paddles (maker, model, weight, shape and purchase date)
  • AI coach answers

AI coach

Only when you tap the AI coach button, the app sends numbers worked out on your phone (joint angles, contact point, score and shot summaries and so on) with your level, dominant hand and app language. For the form review of a practice video, it may also send up to 4 still images: ready position, backswing, contact and follow-through. Videos are never sent. For matches and weekly or monthly recaps, only numbers are sent.

The advice is written by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, through Amazon Bedrock on Amazon Web Services (AWS). Still images are deleted as soon as the advice is ready, and deleted automatically within a day if anything is missed.

Purchases

Pickle Up Premium and 10 AI Coach sessions are bought through the App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details, such as card numbers. Purchases are managed with RevenueCat, and we keep each account’s plan and remaining sessions. RevenueCat receives the account ID we assign and the purchase records.

Usage statistics

To see which features are used across all players, and so decide what to improve, the app sends anonymous statistics. We send only how many times a day a fixed set of features was used (such as starting a recording, importing a video or logging practice), with the app version, the operating system, the app language and the date (without the time). When we test two versions of a screen, the name of the version you see is sent too.

We never send your name, email address, account ID, device ID, advertising ID, location, videos, the content of your records or anything you type. We don’t link the statistics to your account or your phone.

Usage statistics are on by default. Turn off Send usage statistics in Settings › Privacy and data to stop sending them; anything not yet sent is deleted too.

Connection logs and usage analysis

To run the service and keep it secure, we keep the IP address of each connection for up to 30 days. We estimate a rough location (country, region and city) from it and use only totals that identify no one to understand usage and improve the service. To count monthly users, IP addresses are kept during that month only in a form that can’t be reversed (hashed with a monthly secret); at the end of the month the secret is deleted, so they can no longer be linked to anyone. If we ever want to use your location in a way linked to you, such as a local campaign, we will ask for your consent first.

To improve the service, we also analyse how each account is used (days used, the number of records created, AI coach use and plan status), without names or email addresses. If you delete your account, this data can no longer be linked to it.

Our server logs never contain personal content such as your notes, what is sent to the AI, or images.

Notifications

Practice reminders and analysis-finished alerts are created and shown by your phone itself. Nothing is sent to our servers for them.

4. Service providers and where data is kept

We use the following companies to run the app.

Amazon Web Services, Inc.Accounts, record backups, AI coach requests, and storing and processing usage statistics. Data is kept in the Singapore region. Verification emails are also sent through AWS.
Anthropic, PBC (through Amazon Bedrock)Writing AI coach advice. Processing may take place in any AWS region worldwide, not only Singapore.
RevenueCat, Inc.Managing purchases made on the App Store and Google Play
Apple Inc. and Google LLCDistributing the app, purchases and payment, and signing in with Apple or Google (if you choose to)

Information handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat is subject to their own privacy policies.

For what happens when you delete your account, see section 9.

6. Access to your phone’s features

CameraTo film matches and practice
MicrophoneTo record sound with your videos, hear paddle hits, listen for “Start” and “Stop”, and record voice-overs
Speech recognitionTo recognise “Start” and “Stop” on the phone itself (only if you use it)
PhotosTo bring in videos filmed outside the app, and to save videos you choose to your Photos app or gallery
NotificationsTo show practice reminders and let you know when analysis has finished

Each is used only if you allow it. You can change this at any time in your phone’s Settings.

7. People in your videos, and face blur

Your videos may show the people you play with and others nearby. Please get their agreement before you film them, and before you share a video.

When you share a video or highlight, the app can make a copy with other people’s faces blurred. Blurring is on by default, and your own face is left clear. You can change this under Face blur in Settings › Privacy and data, and choose again each time you share. Faces are located from the skeletons found during analysis, so people whose skeleton isn’t found, such as those far away, may not be blurred. Please check before you share. Your original video, and videos saved to your Photos app, are not changed.

8. Sharing and third parties

Videos and images are shared or saved to your Photos app only when you do so, and only to where you choose.

We never sell information from the app, and we never use it for advertising or tracking. The app contains no third-party tools for advertising or for tracking what you do. Apart from the providers in section 4, we don’t give information from the app to anyone, unless the law requires us to.

9. Deleting your information

Videos

  • One at a time: delete a video from its screen. For a video with analysis, choose Delete video only to remove just the video file and keep watching its skeletons and shots without the video.
  • Several at once: tap Select in the video library or a folder, then delete the videos you pick.
  • Free up space: in Settings › Privacy and data, choose Free up space and pick large or old videos to delete. Videos with analysis lose only the video file; their analysis and records stay. Videos without analysis are deleted with their record.
  • Folders: when you delete a folder, choose Delete folder only (keep videos), or delete the folder and the videos in it.

All data

Delete all data, in Settings › Privacy and data, removes every video, record and setting from this phone. If you are signed in, the records saved to your account are not deleted and come back at the next sync. To delete them from our servers too, delete your account. Videos saved to your Photos app stay there.

Your account

Delete account, in Settings › Account, deletes your account with its records, AI coach results and remaining sessions on our servers. The anonymous account the app created for you can be deleted the same way. This account’s videos, records and settings on this phone are deleted too.

  • Your data on our servers is deleted shortly after we receive the request.
  • To understand why people stop using the app, we keep usage data that can’t be traced to you for 2 years: match and practice figures, plan and usage counts and so on. We don’t keep names, notes, anything you typed, email addresses or IDs. If you sign up again, even with the same email address, you get a new account that isn’t linked to this data.
  • So that nothing is written back to a deleted account, we keep only its ID and the deletion date for about 400 days.
  • A paid plan is not cancelled automatically. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.
  • To have your purchase records deleted from RevenueCat, please contact us.

Deleting the app from your phone also removes what it stored there. Videos saved to your Photos app stay there.

10. Children

Children should use the app with a parent’s or guardian’s consent. If you think a child’s information has been sent to us without that consent, please contact us and we will delete it once we have checked.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we will post the update on this page. If a change significantly affects how your information is handled, we will also tell you in the app and ask you to agree again.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy, or to ask to see, correct or delete your information, please use the contact form.
