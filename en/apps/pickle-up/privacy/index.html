Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Quests Bay Pte. Ltd. (“we”) handles information in the pickle-up app for smartphones (“the app”). For how we handle personal data in general, see our privacy policy.

1. In short

  • The videos you film with the app, and the results of analysing them, are stored only on your phone.
  • We do not receive these videos or results. The app does not communicate with our servers.
  • The app contains no third-party tools for advertising or for analysing how you use it.

2. Information the app handles

The app keeps the following in its own storage on your phone:

  • The videos you film, with their sound
  • The results of analysing them (estimated body positions, ball positions, rallies, scores, and the positions and heights of shots)
  • Your practice log, notes, the profile you entered when you started, and the app’s settings

All analysis runs on the device, without any network connection. To tell players apart, it uses where the people in the video stand and the colours of their clothes. It does not recognise faces or identify anyone.

3. Access to your phone’s features

CameraTo film matches and practice
MicrophoneTo record sound with your videos
PhotosTo bring in videos filmed outside the app, and to save videos you choose to the Photos app
NotificationsTo show the practice reminders you set, from the device itself

Each is used only if you allow it. You can change this at any time in your phone’s Settings.

4. People in your videos

Your videos may show the people you play with and others nearby. Please get their agreement before you film them, and before you share a video.

5. Sharing and third parties

Videos are shared or saved to the Photos app only when you do so, and only to where you choose. We never give information from the app to third parties.

Information that Apple Inc. or Google LLC collect when you download or update the app is handled under their own privacy policies.

6. Deleting your information

You can delete a video from its screen at any time. Delete all data in the Me tab removes everything the app has stored. Deleting the app from your phone also removes what it stored. Videos you saved to the Photos app stay there.

7. Children

The app has no way for us to receive information from children. Children should use it with a parent’s or guardian’s consent.

8. Changes to this policy

Before we add features that change how information is handled, such as signing in or advice from AI, we will update this policy and announce it in the app and on this page.
