Pickle Up support

If something isn’t working as you expect, start with the questions below. If they don’t help, send us a message through the contact form.

Getting started

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone and Android versions are on the way. We will post on this site when they are out.

Do I need to sign in?

No. The first time you use the AI coach, buy something or back up your records, the app creates an anonymous account for you.

Filming

Where do I put my phone?

Behind the court and as high as you can. Frame it a little wide, with all four corners and the server on your side in view. A corner or the side works too, as long as all four corners are in view. A tripod or a fence mount helps.

  • All four corners in view
  • Near players fully in view
Do I need to say whether it’s a match or practice?

No. The app works it out from the video. If it gets it wrong, you can change it on the video’s screen.

Can I save a video to my Photos app?

Yes. Save it to your phone’s photos from the video’s screen.

Score and calls

Does the app keep the score?

Yes. It splits your recorded match into rallies and keeps the score. During a match, it calls the score after each rally.

Can the score be called in Japanese?

Yes. Calls are in English by default, as pickleball calls are in English on any court. Switch to Japanese under Voice language in the Record tab.

How do I turn the calls off?

Turn off Call the score aloud in the Record tab. The score is still kept without them.

Analysis

Where are my videos analysed?

On your phone. Your videos are never uploaded to analyse skeletons, the ball, rallies or the score.

How accurate is the analysis?

It is estimated from one phone’s video, so landings on the far side and ball heights are off by a little. Shots it cannot judge with confidence are not drawn; they are counted as unclear.

How do I see my own shots?

Open the match’s video, choose You, and tap your box in a frame where everyone is in view. Your shots are found rally by rally and shown in the Insights tab.

What does “Unclear” mean?

A shot whose landing, or whether it was in or out, can’t be judged with enough certainty. It is counted but not drawn.

How is the height over the net measured?

The shot’s flight is estimated in 3D from the video. Where it passes over the net, we measure from the bottom of the ball to the top of the net. For a ball that hits the net, the height where it met the net is shown as a negative number.

The court is in the wrong place

Open Court position on the video’s screen and move the corners. The video is analysed again with the corrected court.

What does the AI coach send?

Only numbers worked out on your phone (your shots, rallies, score and so on). No videos or images are sent.

Practice

What drills are there?

The Practice tab has 25 drills, grouped by level on the USA Pickleball scale, so you can pick ones that suit you.

Can I log my practice?

Yes. Use Log practice to record the drills you did and for how long.

Account and data

What happens when I change phones?

Your records and analysis results are backed up to your account. On your old phone, create an account in Settings › Account › Create account / Sign in. Sign in with it on your new phone and your records come back. Video files don’t move across, so save the ones you want to keep to your Photos app.

Where are my videos kept?

In the app’s own storage on your phone. Videos are never sent to our servers. Only your records and analysis results are saved to your account.

How do I free up space?

Use Free up space in Settings › Privacy and data to delete large or old videos in one go. Videos with analysis lose only the video file; their analysis and records stay.

How do I delete videos?

Delete one from its screen, or tap Select in the video library or a folder to delete several at once. For a video with analysis, choose Delete video only to keep its skeletons and shots without the video.

Does deleting a folder delete its videos?

You choose. Delete folder only (keep videos) leaves the videos. Deleting the folder and its videos removes them too.

How do I delete all my data?

Use Delete all data in Settings › Privacy and data. It removes every video, record and setting from this phone. If you’re signed in, your account’s records are kept and come back at the next sync.

How do I delete my account?

Use Delete account in Settings › Account. Your records on our servers are deleted too. A paid plan isn’t cancelled automatically, so cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

How do I stop usage statistics?

Turn off Send usage statistics in Settings › Privacy and data. Only anonymous counts are sent, such as how many times a day each feature is used. Nothing that identifies you is sent.

Can I blur faces in videos I share?

Yes. When you share, other people’s faces are blurred automatically. To blur your own face too, turn on Blur my face too in Settings › Privacy and data. Some faces, such as people far away, may not be blurred, so please check before you share.

Can I add my paddles?

Yes. Add the maker, model, weight, shape and purchase date under Paddles in the Settings tab. Your paddle is then added to new videos and to practice and match records.

Plans and purchases

Is it free?

Yes. Recording, automatic scoring and calls, rally splitting, the drills and practice logs are free. Shot analysis is free for 60 minutes of video a month and the AI coach twice a month; Premium makes shot analysis unlimited and the AI coach available up to 50 times a month.

What does Premium add?

Shot analysis for every video, and the AI coach up to 50 times a month. Choose monthly or yearly.

How do I use my purchases on a new phone?

Sign in with the same account. You can also use Restore purchases in Settings › Plan to bring back a plan you bought before.

How do I cancel?

Use Manage or cancel subscription in Settings › Plan to cancel in the App Store or Google Play. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel it.

Contact us

Report a problem, ask how something works, or send us your ideas. Sending opens your email app with your message filled in.

Find it in the app under Settings › Help and about. Opened from the app’s Contact button, it is filled in for you.

We handle your message under our privacy policy.
