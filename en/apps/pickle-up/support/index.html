Support

pickle-up support

If something is not working as you expect, start with the questions below. If they do not help, send us a message through the contact form.

Filming

Where should I put my phone?

Behind the baseline, as high as you can. Frame it a little wide, so all four corners and the player waiting to serve behind the near line are in view. A tripod or a fence mount helps.

Can I film from the front, by the net?

Please film from behind or from the side: a shot could hit your phone. The analysis expects video taken from behind the court.

Do I need to say whether it is a match or practice?

No. The app works it out from the video. If it gets it wrong, you can change it on the video’s screen.

Analysis

How do I see my own shots?

Open the match’s video, choose You, and tap your box in a frame where everyone is in view. Your shots are found rally by rally and shown in the Insights tab.

What does “unclear” mean?

A shot whose landing, or whether it was in or out, cannot be judged with enough certainty. It is counted but not drawn.

How is the height from the net measured?

The shot’s flight is estimated in 3D from the video. Where it passes over the net, we measure from the bottom of the ball to the top of the net. For a ball that hits the net, the height where it met the net is shown as a negative number.

The court is in the wrong place

Open Court position on the video’s screen and move the corners. The video is analysed again with the corrected court.

Your data

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Videos and results are kept in the app’s own storage on your phone.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a video from its screen. To delete everything, use Delete all data in the Me tab. Videos you saved to the Photos app stay there.

What happens when I change phones?

In this version your data lives only on the phone and does not move to a new one. Save the videos you want to keep to the Photos app.

Contact us

Send us a message through the contact form. Telling us your phone model, its OS version and the app’s version (Version, in the Me tab) helps us answer sooner.
